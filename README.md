# Why I love *The 100* Series
### I love The 100 because it's not just about survival, it's about love, friendship, sacrifice, and humanity. I love how every character has their own story and how their experiences change them. It makes me feel so many emotions, keeps me hooked, and reminds me that even in the darkest situations, people will always find something worth fighting for.
*this is the series that I never get tired watching.*
> Maybe being human isn't about being perfect, but about making mistakes, losing people, and still finding the strength to keep going.
